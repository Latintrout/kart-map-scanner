KART MAP SCANNER

Draw a route on free OSM data, export it for Godot.

1. Find the area

How it works

  1. Find your area (search, or drag the map in ✋ Pan).
  2. Draw the route with ✎ Pen (hold and drag along roads). It snaps to roads.
  3. Scan area: grabs buildings, roads, trees and more around it.
  4. Check the map: red = blocking the track. Try 🧊 3D preview.
  5. Export JSON and drop it in the game's tracks folder.
Extras (colours, heights, photos) are optional. 📏 (top-left of the map) measures distances. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo.

Saved tracks

Saved in this browser only. "Back up all" downloads a file you can restore on another computer or browser.
